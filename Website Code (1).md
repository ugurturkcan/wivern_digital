<!-- Ecological Standards -->
<div class="glass-panel p-10 rounded-sm">
<h3 class="serif-font text-xl text-white tracking-wider mb-4">Ecological Parameters</h3>
<p class="text-gray-400 font-light text-sm leading-relaxed mb-6">


An Ecology Fund, established from a baseline of 15% of all generation receipts, is reinvested into agricultural development initiatives for farmers. Furthermore,
these funds procure the infrastructure required to ensure all processing power utilized in avatar production is sourced exclusively from wind or solar energy. This protocol
ensures a net-zero environmental impact, preventing any net loss in ecological resources during production.
</p>

<!-- Compensation Standards -->
<div class="glass-panel p-10 rounded-sm">
<h3 class="serif-font text-xl text-white tracking-wider mb-4">Equitable Remuneration</h3>
<p class="text-gray-400 font-light text-sm leading-relaxed mb-6">
A fixed compensation structure mandates the distribution of project receipts. Standard avatar production is executed by a single Master Avatarist, who ensures
comprehensive process completion and receives a 33.3% allocation. Apprenticeship positions are uncompensated (0%), while Journeymen receive a 16.6% allocation. In
instances where a Journeyman and a Master collaborate on a single requisition, the standard 19% Operations Reserve (otherwise utilized for software licenses and cloud
hosting) is proportionally decreased to cover the Journeyman's wage. Undistributed capital defaults to the Ecology Fund.
</p>
</div>
</div>
