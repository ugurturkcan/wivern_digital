Wivern Digital: Establishment Protocol

Wivern Digital operates as a guild of digital artists and technologists, focused on the ethical and ecologically sound production
of high-fidelity AI avatars for the commercial market. This framework transitions the traditional agency model into a
collaborative ecosystem, prioritizing digital craftsmanship, environmental regeneration, and equitable profit distribution.


The operational model utilizes a "Master Driver" architecture. In this setup, a singular human performance drives multiple
client avatars. This ensures consistent output and reduces recurring computational demands, aligning with the ecological
commitments of the organization.


Historically, guilds operated on principles of mastery, shared standards, and the protection of trade secrets—all of which are highly relevant in the rapidly evolving
artificial intelligence landscape. Wivern Digital updates this historical framework for the modern digital age, functioning as a collaborative ecosystem focused on
the ethical and ecologically sound production of high-fidelity AI avatars for the commercial market.


To establish this structure for AI avatar production, the organization relies on specific foundational pillars. The primary pillar is the establishment of the "Mastery"
Hierarchy. Replacing standard corporate titles with traditional progression creates a clear path for both the technical team and commercial patrons:


Apprentices: Entry-level AI programmers utilizing basic automated tools. Serve for three months and must be voted by Journeymen and Masters to advance.

Journeymen: Skilled Ai practitioners who have mastered the foundational formulas and can customize AI outputs for specific commercial client needs.

Masters: The elite practitioners who develop the underlying models, specialized prompts, and unique digital assets, such as custom LoRAs or checkpoints.


Revenue Distribution Model:


Founder (Master-in-Chief): 33.3% – Allocated for domain authority, diologue vetting, and platform stewardship.

The Avatarists: 33.3% – Compensation for model tuning, prompting, client acquisition and output refinement.

The Ecology Fund: 15% – Dedicated entirely to carbon-negative compute and nature restoration projects.

Guild Operations: 19% – Reserved for software licenses, cloud hosting, and common pool distributions for members.


The Funds


The 33.3 percent Avatarist fund is designated for the Avatarist responsible for the project, where the exact distribution of
this share is scaled according to the practitioner's mastery level. When a “Master” alone executes a project, they receive
the full 33.3 percent artist allocation. When a “Journeyman” completes a project, they receive 16.6% of that allocation,
with the remainder diverted to the guild operations pool to cover the advanced tools provided by the Masters. An
“Apprentice” must learn the systems for 3 month and be voted into the Guild by a majority of the Masters and
Journeymen to join. If a Master and Journeyman both work on a project, the Guild Operations fund is diluted to cover
the Journeyman’s share. This tiered approach ensures that higher levels of craftsmanship and proprietary contribution
are financially rewarded while sustaining the infrastructure required for training lower-tier members.



Step-by-Step Establishment Guide


Phase 1: Digital Infrastructure and Brand Deployment


Domain and Website Deployment: Route the Wivern domain through a DNS provider to a hosting platform like Vercel or AWS Amplify.
Code the front-end interface utilizing a framework such as Next.js or React.

Payment Integration: Integrate a payment gateway, such as Stripe, directly into the platform to facilitate straightforward commercial
transactions and client intake.

Server Architecture Configuration: Establish an account on a GPU cloud provider, such as RunPod or Lambda Labs, specifically targeting
data centers verified to operate on renewable energy (e.g., Iceland or Quebec). Rent an instance equipped with an NVIDIA H100 GPU (80
gigabytes of VRAM) for initial training, and configure a more cost-effective NVIDIA L40S or RTX 5090 instance for continuous daily
generation.




Step-by-Step Establishment Guide


Phase 2: The Proprietary AI Pipeline Configuration


Core Model Installation: Connect the rented cloud instance to the Hugging Face repository to download the open-weight Wan2.2 models, the
DreamActor V2 or LivePortrait frameworks, and the necessary audio-driven lip-sync nodes (e.g., Seedance 2.0). Compile these into a
ComfyUI node-based workflow on the server for visual pipeline management.

Voice Synthesis and Cloning Setup: Integrate a Text-to-Speech (TTS) framework. Utilize an API such as ElevenLabs or deploy an open-
source alternative like GPT-SoVITS directly on the server. Input a clean audio sample from the target identity to clone the specific vocal
timbre and speech patterns.

Motion Capture Procedure: Record the source actor using a 4K camera in a studio environment utilizing soft, diffuse lighting, ensuring no
hard shadows are cast across the face.

Data Extraction: Upload the 4K footage to the cloud server where a Python-based 3D Variational Autoencoder extracts the facial landmarks
and upper-face muscle movements. Export and save this data as reusable ".pkl" or ".json" template files to form the master driver library.

Lip-Sync and Audio Mapping: Route the generated TTS audio file into the ComfyUI workflow. Map the audio phonemes to the lower-face
muscle movements utilizing the LivePortrait or Seedance node, ensuring precise synchronization between the synthesized voice and the
avatar's lips.

Temporal Consistency Application: Insert a temporal consistency layer into the rendering pipeline to smooth frame-to-frame transitions. This
process eliminates visual jitter and stabilizes the generated output prior to final rendering.

Identity Swap Validation: Upload a static, high-resolution portrait of a test subject into the ComfyUI workflow. Map the saved master driver
template and the audio-driven lip movements onto the static image. Analyze the resulting output frame-by-frame to confirm identity
preservation and verify the absence of visual glitches.




Step-by-Step Establishment Guide


Phase 3: Commercial Pipeline Operations and Fulfillment


Automated Client Intake: Direct commercial clients to the web platform to upload their source photography and audio scripts. The system
must capture this data and securely store the assets in a cloud bucket prior to processing.

API Handoff and Task Queueing: Configure the front-end web application to communicate directly with the cloud GPU server via an API
interface. Upon successful payment verification, the system must automatically inject the client's source files into the ComfyUI rendering
queue without manual intervention.

First-Pass Automated Rendering: The cloud instance executes the core pipeline, processing the Text-to-Speech generation, the LivePortrait
motion mapping, and the temporal consistency pass to generate a raw, base-level avatar video.

Human-in-the-Loop Refinement: A designated guild artist retrieves the raw video output. The artist manually addresses any generated
artifacts, synchronizes minor lip-sync discrepancies, and applies the established color grading, ensuring the lighting remains soft and diffuse as
per visual standards, to guarantee commercial fidelity.

Secondary Rendering and Upscaling: The refined video is processed through a secondary command-line tool, such as Topaz Video AI, to
upscale the footage to 4K resolution and conform the frame rate to 60 frames per second.

Ecological Ledger Processing: The server script calculates the exact GPU compute time utilized for the specific client project. This metric is
logged in the internal ledger to quantify the required carbon offset and document the 15 percent ecology fund allocation.

Secure Delivery and Archiving: The finalized 4K asset is delivered to the client via a secure, encrypted download link. The client's unique
visual identity and associated data are archived within the proprietary database for future, rapid-turnaround commercial campaigns.
